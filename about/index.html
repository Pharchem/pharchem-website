Pharchem Private Limited is a specialised manufacturer of non-sterile pharmaceutical APIs and fine chemicals, supplying Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste and Magnesium Hydroxide Paste to leading Indian and global pharmaceutical companies since 1989.

Our Story

Founded in 1989 in the Tarapur MIDC industrial belt at Boisar, Palghar district, Maharashtra, Pharchem Private Limited has spent over three decades focused on a narrow, deep specialisation: high-purity Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste and Magnesium Hydroxide Paste for the pharmaceutical industry.

What started as a single-product API manufacturer has grown into a trusted supplier to leading Indian and multinational pharma companies — Pfizer, Abbott, Alkem, Macleods, RPG, Enicar — and to specialty lake colour producers including Roha Dyechem, Neelikon, Akay and Aayusharyan.

Our combined monthly capacity of 610 metric tonnes (450 MT ALP + 160 MT MGP), USP-grade output backed by tightly controlled in-house specifications, and consistent supply track record have earned us a reputation as a reliable, technically capable partner for both branded and generic formulators.

Manufacturing & Quality

Tarapur MIDC (Boisar) facility

Located in the Tarapur M.I.D.C. industrial area at Boisar, Palghar district, Maharashtra — one of India's premier pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing zones. Strategic proximity to JNPT and Nhava Sheva ports for export logistics; Boisar railway station is on the Western Line.

Quality systems

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System and c-GMP Schedule M compliance govern every process — from raw material sourcing through in-process control to finished-batch release.

Pharmacopoeial compliance

Both products are tested against the USP monograph and our internal release specifications. Each consignment is accompanied by a Certificate of Analysis. DMF and regulatory documentation available on request.

Long-term partnerships

Multi-decade supply relationships with several of our customers attest to our consistency and dependability — qualities that matter most in API supply chains.
