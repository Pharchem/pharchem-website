FAQ — Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste

What is Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste?

Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste (ALP) is a non-sterile pharmaceutical API consisting of a wet gel of aluminium hydroxide [Al(OH)3] suspended in water. It is supplied at IP/BP/USP grade with an Al2O3 assay of 8%–12%.

What is ALP used for?

ALP's two main uses are: (1) as the active ingredient in liquid antacid formulations that neutralise stomach acid, and (2) as the foundational substrate for FD&C lake colour pigments used in pharma, food and cosmetic applications. It is also used in vaccine adjuvants and phosphate-binder formulations.

What is the difference between ALP paste and dried Aluminium Hydroxide Gel?

ALP is the wet paste form (high water content, ~88–92% solids depending on grade). Dried Aluminium Hydroxide Gel is the spray-dried or oven-dried powder derived from the paste, with a higher Al2O3 assay (typically 47–60%). The wet paste is used in liquid antacids; the dried powder in tablet and chewable formulations.
