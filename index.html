Applications & Industries Served

Our Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste and Magnesium Hydroxide Paste are trusted across pharmaceutical and food-colour industries.

Liquid Antacid Suspensions

ALP and MGP are the active ingredients in widely prescribed liquid antacids that neutralise stomach acid and treat hyperacidity, GERD and peptic ulcers.

Milk of Magnesia & Laxatives

Magnesium Hydroxide Paste is the foundational API in milk-of-magnesia formulations and saline laxative products used worldwide.

Lake Colour Substrates

Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste is the foundational substrate for FD&C lake colours used in pharmaceuticals, food, cosmetics and confectionery.

Vaccine Adjuvants

Specialised Aluminium Hydroxide Gel grades are used as adjuvants in vaccine formulations to enhance immune response.

Phosphate Binders

Aluminium Hydroxide is used in pharmaceutical phosphate-binder formulations for patients with chronic kidney disease.

Cosmetic & Personal Care

High-purity Magnesium Hydroxide is used in deodorants, skincare and personal-care formulations as a buffering and absorbent agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our APIs.

What is Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste?

Aluminium Hydroxide Gel Paste (ALP) is a high-purity, non-sterile pharmaceutical API used primarily as the active ingredient in liquid antacid formulations and as the foundational substrate for FD&C lake colours. Pharchem manufactures ALP at Pharma Grade & In-House specification with an Al2O3 assay of 8%–12% and a monthly capacity of 450 MT.

What is Magnesium Hydroxide Paste used for?

Magnesium Hydroxide Paste (MGP) is widely used as the active ingredient in antacid suspensions and laxative formulations. It neutralises gastric acid and is often combined with Aluminium Hydroxide Gel for balanced antacid efficacy. Pharchem's MGP is supplied at Pharma Grade & In-House specification with a Mg(OH)2 assay of 19%–35%.

Are your APIs certified for export?

Yes. Pharchem operates under an ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System and complies with c-GMP Schedule M requirements. Our APIs meet the USP monograph and our internal release specifications, and are supplied to leading pharmaceutical manufacturers across India and international markets.
